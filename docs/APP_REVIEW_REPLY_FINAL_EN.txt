Hello App Review Team,

I own and develop Namaz Grubum. Here are answers to all nine questions before resubmission.

1. Purpose and problem
Namaz Grubum helps friends coordinate prayer participation, especially waking for morning prayer. Members join groups, record "I am awake" and "I have prayed," see participation, and send member wake-up reminders. Owners manage admission and membership. Optional camera submissions are restricted to active members and normally expire after 12 hours. The problem is shared follow-through beyond an individual alarm.

2. Intended users
Turkish-speaking Muslims who already pray but struggle with morning or other prayers and want voluntary support from friends, family, or community members. Organizers manage groups; members acknowledge waking, record completion, and encourage one another.

3. Specific value
A timetable answers "When is prayer?" This app answers "Who in our group has responded, and who may need a reminder?" Membership, prayer-specific shared states, reminders, and optional photos work together. A chat thread requires members to reconstruct that record manually. I am describing this integrated workflow, not claiming no comparable feature exists elsewhere.

4. Beta testing and applied feedback
Internal TestFlight records show two installed tester accounts: mine and one additional tester, on iPhone SE (2nd generation) and iPhone 14 Pro Max. There are 17 feedback records, not 17 testers: 14 mine and three from the additional tester, dated September 10-October 2, 2026.
Applied feedback included persistent onboarding completion, separating a crowded group title/role badge, adding a timeout and outcome states to joining, hiding "report own group" for owners/admins, and clearer three-second wake-up confirmation.
The rejected version is linked to build 8. Later work is in TestFlight builds 9-10; I do not attribute later changes to build 8. Build 10 is VALID and passed 31 automated tests.

5. Standalone product and other apps
This is standalone, not a suite or branded variant. My closest related app, "Kolay Namaz Ile Namaza Basla" (6806850484), supports people starting or returning to prayer: one daily prayer, individual guidance, and mentor support. Namaz Grubum serves people already praying through shared group participation. Prayer times/reminders overlap; the primary workflows differ. Other account products cover books, goal-based alarms, travel, a matcha journal, gifts, chat analysis, and receipts, not this group service.

6. Why separate from Kolay Namaz?
Group features could technically be added there. I separated them because beginners need gradual personal guidance, while practicing friends need invitations, admission, shared records, member reminders, and moderation as the main experience. Neither product requires the other; this is not an additional lesson or content pack.

7. Sharing with my other apps
The applications were developed separately. I did not transfer first-party code, screens, logos, or content from my other apps. Standard dependencies such as Flutter and Firebase may recur.

8. Third-party components and origin
I developed the code with AI assistance, not by purchasing a completed app to rebrand.
